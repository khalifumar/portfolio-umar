## Executive Summary

An exhaustive retail business intelligence and financial performance analysis spanning **9,900+ transaction records** across US states. This study utilized modern spreadsheet engineering and exploratory data modeling to detect consumer buying behaviors, logistics bottlenecks, and regional profit leakages.

## Key Deliverables

- **Data Transformation Pipeline via Power Query:**
  Constructed an automated data sanitization and ETL workflow in Microsoft Excel to ingest raw transaction dumps, handle missing geographical fields, and resolve currency formats.
- **Dynamic Multi-Dimensional Dashboards:**
  Engineered interactive Excel dashboards featuring slicers, dynamic named ranges, and Pivot Charts to visualize state-level revenues, category profit margins, and discount sensitivities.
- **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) & Root Cause Diagnostics:**
  Conducted granular statistical evaluations uncovering that certain high-volume categories suffered negative net margins due to aggressive regional discounting and freight surcharges.

## Strategic Recommendations

- Realigned discount thresholds for heavy freight categories, converting loss-leader product lines into profitable bundles.
- Prioritized high-margin regional hubs to optimize logistics delivery times and inventory allocation.

## Tools & Techniques

- **Platform:** Microsoft Excel (Advanced)
- **Features:** Power Query ETL, Power Pivot, DAX Calculated Columns, Dynamic Slicers, What-If Sensitivity Tables
