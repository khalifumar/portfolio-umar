## Executive Summary

The **Business Analytics & Reporting Suite** is an enterprise data infrastructure and reporting solution designed to unify siloed business data across operations, inventory, and procurement. By architecting centralized relational data models and dynamic KPI monitoring, executive leadership gained immediate visibility into high-impact operational metrics.

## Key Engineering & Analytical Deliverables

- **Centralized Operational Data Models & Relational Schemas:**
  Aggregated cross-departmental records across sales, inventory movements, and procurement to eliminate data duplication and establish a verified Single Source of Truth (SSOT).
- **Executive KPI Monitoring & Real-Time Analytics:**
  Engineered dynamic analytical dashboards tracking critical performance metrics—including gross profit margins, inventory stock turnover rates, and order velocity across multiple operational tiers.
- **Pipeline Data Validation & Integrity Synchronization:**
  Conducted transactional log sanitization, constraints enforcement, and pipeline synchronization to ensure data accuracy across high-volume production tables.

## Business Impact

- Reduced recurring operational reporting time from hours to instantaneous automated refreshes.
- Empowered department leads with demand forecasting models to reduce dead-stock risk and procurement costs.

## Technical Architecture

- **Database:** PostgreSQL / Supabase
- **Data Modeling:** Star Schema / Snowflake relational architecture, Kimball dimensional modeling
- **Analytics & Querying:** Advanced SQL (Window functions, CTEs, indexing, query execution optimization)
- **Visualization:** Interactive Web UI (Next.js / React) with real-time KPI charting
