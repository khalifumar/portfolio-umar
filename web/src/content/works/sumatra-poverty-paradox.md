## Executive Summary

A data-driven policy investigation into the socioeconomic dynamics across Sumatra’s provinces. This project analyzed the counterintuitive divergence between low reported open unemployment rates and persistent poverty indices—termed the **"Sumatra Poverty Paradox."**

## Analytical Process

- **Data Ingestion & Multi-Source Cleansing:**
  Aggregated multi-year regional census figures, provincial GDP breakdowns, employment quality metrics, and poverty ratios across Sumatra.
- **Statistical Dissection of Underemployment:**
  Demonstrated through statistical correlation that low official unemployment masked a high concentration of vulnerable, low-productivity informal sector employment and seasonal agricultural labor lacking social safety nets.
- **Interactive Storytelling via Looker Studio:**
  Designed spatial map benchmarks, provincial time-series trends, and narrative callouts for policymakers and regional development researchers.

## Actionable Takeaways

- Shifted focus from merely targeting raw unemployment rates to monitoring labor productivity and social protection coverage in rural agrarian sectors.
- Provided an interactive framework for public sector resource allocation based on multi-dimensional poverty indices.

## Technology Stack

- **Dashboard:** Google Looker Studio
- **Data Preparation:** SQL (PostgreSQL), Microsoft Excel Power Query
- **Analytical Methods:** Bivariate Correlation, Spatial Mapping, Demographic Segmentation
