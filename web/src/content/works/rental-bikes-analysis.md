## Executive Summary

The **Rental Bikes Demand Analysis** is an interactive analytical web application developed to explore patterns, seasonality, and environmental determinants governing urban bike sharing systems.

## Methodology & Analysis

- **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):**
  Uncovered distinct utilization profiles between registered commuters (peaking sharply during morning and evening rush hours) versus casual leisure riders (heavily concentrated on weekend afternoons).
- **Environmental Factor Modeling:**
  Evaluated the impact of temperature, humidity, windspeed, and weather classifications on overall ridership volume, establishing clear thresholds where demand drops precipitously.
- **Interactive Streamlit Web Dashboard:**
  Deployed real-time date filtering, seasonal comparisons, and interactive seaborn/matplotlib visualizations allowing municipal planners and fleet managers to simulate operational adjustments.

## Practical Insights

- Recommended dynamic bike rebalancing schedules during morning peak hours (7:00 AM - 9:00 AM) to prevent dock station exhaustion in commercial districts.
- Provided weather-conditioned demand forecasting ranges to optimize field maintenance schedules during low-ridership windows.
